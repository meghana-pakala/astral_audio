The Astro-Logic

Music recommendation today is a backwards-looking system. Streaming services know your listening history and optimize for your past self, which makes them bad at the one thing you actually want from music: meeting you where you are right now.

The missing variable is a predictive measure of emotional state. Music serves as a medium for sharing the human experience — astrology evolved as a system for mapping that experience to the cosmos.

Musica Universalis

Pythagoras proposed that the Sun, Moon and planets each emit a unique hum based on their orbital motion — that the universe has a celestial sound, imperceptible but omnipresent.

The sound is in our ears from the very moment of birth and is thus indistinguishable from its contrary silence. What happens to men, then, is just what happens to coppersmiths, who are so accustomed to the noise of the smithy that it makes no difference to them.

— Aristotle, De Caelo

The concept of musica universalis — harmony of the spheres — holds that mathematical relationships express "tones" of energy manifesting in angles, shapes, and sounds, all connected within a pattern of proportion.

Johannes Kepler formalized this in Harmonices Mundi (1619), demonstrating that the same ratios governing musical consonance define the geometric relationships between planetary positions. A trine at 120° is a perfect fifth. A square at 90° sits at the boundary between consonance and dissonance. The geometry of the sky and the structure of sound are bridged by the same underlying mathematics.

From Stars to Sonics

Astrology operates in the framework of correlation not causation — the same bet that every predictive model makes. We are each a repository of memories and experiences with no dashboard and no readout. Amidst the static of the universe, we are challenged to discover patterns and chart our own paths.

We are born at a given moment, in a given place and, like vintage years of wine, we have the qualities of the year and of the season of which we are born. Astrology does not lay claim to anything more.

— Carl Jung

The ancient philosophers posited that there exists a cosmic energy that affects us all differently depending where in the universe the planets were at the moment we entered the material world. Astral Audio leverages the natural harmonic ratios to map your personal astrological transits and find the songs that match your sky today.

Mapping stars to sonics is not a science, but perhaps when the silence feels louder than usual, the solution lies in tuning your own cosmic harmony.
